基础教材与公开课程

  • Michael A. Nielsen and Isaac L. Chuang, Quantum Computation and Quantum Information, Cambridge University Press, 2010。
  • John Watrous, The Theory of Quantum Information, Cambridge University Press, 2018。
  • John Watrous, Basics of Quantum Information，持续维护的公开课程。
  • John Preskill, Quantum Computation lecture notes。
  • Umesh Vazirani et al., Berkeley CS 191 / Quantum Computation notes。

算法与复杂度

  • D. Deutsch and R. Jozsa, Rapid solution of problems by quantum computation, Proceedings of the Royal Society A 439, 553–558 (1992)。
  • E. Bernstein and U. Vazirani, Quantum complexity theory, SIAM Journal on Computing 26, 1411–1473 (1997)。
  • D. R. Simon, On the Power of Quantum Computation, SIAM Journal on Computing 26, 1474–1483 (1997)。
  • P. W. Shor, Algorithms for quantum computation: Discrete logarithms and factoring, Proceedings of the 35th Annual Symposium on Foundations of Computer Science, 124–134 (1994)。
  • L. K. Grover, A fast quantum mechanical algorithm for database search, Proceedings of the 28th Annual ACM Symposium on Theory of Computing, 212–219 (1996)。
  • G. Brassard, P. Høyer, M. Mosca and A. Tapp, Quantum Amplitude Amplification and Estimation, Contemporary Mathematics 305, 53–74 (2002)。
  • S. Lloyd, Universal quantum simulators, Science 273, 1073–1078 (1996)。
  • A. Peruzzo et al., A variational eigenvalue solver on a photonic quantum processor, Nature Communications 5, 4213 (2014)。
  • E. Farhi, J. Goldstone and S. Gutmann, A Quantum Approximate Optimization Algorithm, arXiv:1411.4028 (2014)。
  • M. Cerezo et al., Variational quantum algorithms, Nature Reviews Physics 3, 625–644 (2021)。

噪声、纠错与容错

  • P. W. Shor, Scheme for reducing decoherence in quantum computer memory, Physical Review A 52, R2493–R2496 (1995)。
  • A. M. Steane, Error Correcting Codes in Quantum Theory, Physical Review Letters 77, 793–797 (1996)。
  • E. Knill and R. Laflamme, Theory of quantum error-correcting codes, Physical Review A 55, 900–911 (1997)。
  • D. Gottesman, Stabilizer Codes and Quantum Error Correction, PhD thesis, California Institute of Technology (1997)。
  • D. Aharonov and M. Ben-Or, Fault-Tolerant Quantum Computation With Constant Error Rate, SIAM Journal on Computing 38, 1207–1282 (2008；早期稿 1997)。
  • B. Eastin and E. Knill, Restrictions on Transversal Encoded Quantum Gate Sets, Physical Review Letters 102, 110502 (2009)。
  • S. Bravyi and A. Kitaev, Universal quantum computation with ideal Clifford gates and noisy ancillas, Physical Review A 71, 022316 (2005)。
  • B. M. Terhal, Quantum error correction for quantum memories, Reviews of Modern Physics 87, 307–346 (2015)。
  • A. G. Fowler et al., Surface codes: Towards practical large-scale quantum computation, Physical Review A 86, 032324 (2012)。

硬件与基准

  • D. P. DiVincenzo, The Physical Implementation of Quantum Computation, Fortschritte der Physik 48, 771–783 (2000)。
  • T. D. Ladd et al., Quantum computers, Nature 464 (2010)。
  • J. M. Gambetta, J. M. Chow, M. Steffen, Building logical qubits in a superconducting quantum computing system, npj Quantum Information 3, 2 (2017)。
  • E. Magesan et al., Scalable and robust randomized benchmarking, PRL 106 (2011)。
  • A. W. Cross et al., Validating quantum computers using randomized model circuits, PRA 100 (2019)。

  • H. J. Manetsch et al., A tweezer array with 6,100 highly coherent atomic qubits, Nature 647, 60–67 (2025)。
  • M. A. Guidry et al., A manufacturable platform for photonic quantum computing, Nature 641, 876–883 (2025)。
  • H. F. Legg, On the robustness of topological gap detection via transport, Nature 654, E22–E26 (2026)；Microsoft Quantum et al., Reply to: On the robustness of topological gap detection via transport, Nature 654, E27–E28 (2026)。
  • N. van Loo et al., Single-shot parity readout of a minimal Kitaev chain, Nature 650, 334–339 (2026；2026-02-11 在线)。

密码与网络

  • NIST, Post-Quantum Cryptography project。
  • NIST, FIPS 203（ML-KEM，密钥封装）、FIPS 204（ML-DSA，数字签名）、FIPS 205（SLH-DSA，数字签名），均于 2024-08-13 发布最终版。引用具体算法或参数时，应打开对应标准页；203、204 的页面另列有待未来修订处理的勘误，不等于最终版被撤销（核查于 2026-09-24）。
  • NIST, HQC announced as a backup algorithm for general encryption，2025-03-11；这是选择公告，不是最终 FIPS 发布。
  • NIST, PQC Standardization 与 Selected Algorithms，持续更新；引用 HQC 阶段时记录访问日期。
  • C. H. Bennett and G. Brassard, Quantum cryptography: Public key distribution and coin tossing, Theoretical Computer Science 560, 7–11 (2014 重印；原工作发表于 1984)。
  • H. J. Kimble, The quantum internet, Nature 453 (2008)。
  • C. M. Knaut et al., Entanglement of nanophotonic quantum memory nodes in a telecom network, Nature 629, 573–578 (2024)。
  • S. Kucera et al., Demonstration of quantum network protocols over a 14-km urban fiber link, npj Quantum Information 10, 88 (2024)。
  • W. Kozlowski et al., Architectural Principles for a Quantum Internet, RFC 9340 (2023)，信息性文档。
  • C. Wang et al., Application Scenarios for the Quantum Internet, RFC 9583 (2024)，信息性文档。
